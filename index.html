How it works

Dulce is the easiest way to track gelato specials. We pull in the latest flavours from Messina so you don't have to scroll social media. Open the app to discover what's new, rate what you've tried, and get notified when your favourites return to cabinets. Categories and powerful search make it easy to go back in time and discover hidden gems. When you're craving a throwback, head to the Profile tab to view your favourites - sorted by rank or by the moment you tried them. You can also get notified when your top-rated flavours return to cabinets.
