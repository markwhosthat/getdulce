How it works

Dulce pulls frozen specials from your favourite spots straight off social media so you don't have to scroll. Just open the app to discover what's new, rate what you've tried, and save the ones you love. Intelligent auto-tagging allows you to browse by category, and powerful search lets you dig into the past. When you're craving a throwback, head to the Me tab to revisit your favourites - sorted by rank or by the moment you tried them.
